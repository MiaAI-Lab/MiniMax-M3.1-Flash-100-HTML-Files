A poster is an argument
made in one second

1926 — Dessau
Herbert Bayer
Joost Schmidt
Max Bill

The Bauhaus poster workshop was the only place in Europe where typography, colour and geometry were taught as a single subject. Students arrived with painting skills and left able to design a whole room.

What survives from that workshop is not a style but a procedure: reduce the message, find the grid, choose one primary colour, set the type without apology, and print it at the size of the street.

The studio on the left is a small machine for running that procedure. Change the palette, the scale, the rotation; press Neues Plakat for a new composition drawn from the same vocabulary.
