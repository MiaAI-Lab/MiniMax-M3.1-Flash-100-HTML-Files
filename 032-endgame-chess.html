Endgames are
the honest part
of chess

Most games are decided long before the queens come off. What is left at the end is arithmetic: squares, diagonals, the single square a king cannot be caught on. A composed endgame has one idea, and finding it is the same feeling as reading a good short story.

The studies here are public domain and were chosen for their geometry rather than their difficulty. The first is a nineteenth-century gift; the second is a modern one that takes an afternoon.
