DESCRIPTION:
A room you can hear. The impulse response is built with the image-source method in three dimensions — every mirrored copy of the source across the six walls is placed, its distance gives the delay and its amplitude the reflection coefficient raised to the number of bounces — and that array is loaded into a convolver, so the clap you hear is genuinely the room you have built. The plan view draws the same wavefronts expanding and reflecting, from the same list of images.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Three-dimensional image-source method for a shoebox room
- ConvolverNode loaded with the computed impulse response
- Wavefronts drawn from the image list and clipped to the room plan
- Short decaying noise burst added to every arrival to model a clap
- Sabine reverberation time and critical distance computed from the geometry
- Envelope curve extracted from the impulse response

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Clap. Resize the room, change the wall absorption, and clap again; the tail shortens or lengthens as the room does. The three presets give you a concert hall, a cellar and a cathedral.
