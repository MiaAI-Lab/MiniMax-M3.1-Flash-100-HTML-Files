DESCRIPTION:
A double pendulum that is really simulated. The coupled equations are integrated with fourth-order Runge-Kutta six times a frame, and the energy readout is the honest expression including the cross term, so the drift figure tells you how well the integrator is doing. Drag a bob and let go; the teal trail of the lower bob is the only honest way to see what chaos looks like.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Fourth-order Runge-Kutta integration of the coupled double-pendulum equations
- Full kinetic and potential energy with the cross term, and a live drift readout
- Fading trail of the lower bob rendered segment by segment
- Radial-gradient brass and teal bobs sized by mass
- Pointer dragging that sets the angles and zeroes the velocities
- Faint 40px laboratory grid on the back plate
