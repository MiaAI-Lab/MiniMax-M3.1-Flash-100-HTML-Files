What the
order of
the layers
means

Sediments settle in the order they arrive, and each layer is a snapshot of a landscape. A band of pale sand is a flood; a band of dark clay is a still, deep water; a bed of shell fragments is a sea that came in and went out again.

Read the whole column and you have a coastline that moved. Read it with a magnet in it and you have the reversal of the Earth's field frozen into the floor. Read it with pollen in it and you have what was growing.

Method

The core is drawn procedurally: each stratum is a band whose colour, grain and thickness are drawn from a seeded sequence, and its edges are irregular, because a real bedding plane is never straight. The scan draws a bright marker line down the column and the read panel follows it.
