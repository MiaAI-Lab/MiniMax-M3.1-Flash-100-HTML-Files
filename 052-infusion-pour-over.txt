DESCRIPTION:
A pour-over recipe as a stopwatch. Most brewing guides say "around 30 seconds" and "wait until it drops halfway", which is both kind and useless; this one gives you the exact seconds. Press start and the ring fills over three minutes while the phase name, the step list and the pouring stream all advance together, so you can follow the clock with one hand and the kettle with the other.
