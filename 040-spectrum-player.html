There is no audio file here and nothing is recorded. The spectrum is synthesised: sixty-four bands, each driven by a bank of sines with a shared envelope, then smoothed into the kind of shape a slow, wide piece of music actually makes.

Each track on the list has a different seed, so switching tracks does not just change the title — it changes the entire shape of the sound, which is the honest way to build a visualiser that cannot hear anything.
