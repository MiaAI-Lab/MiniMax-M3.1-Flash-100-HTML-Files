Every pattern
is a
grammar

A pattern is not a picture. It is a rule that you can break without noticing. Six squares, four corners, one diagonal and a decision about which way the eye is supposed to travel — that is enough to generate an infinite family, and the whole history of textile design is the history of people finding new rules inside old ones.

The tiles here use only what CSS can do well: repeating gradients at angles, conic gradients for radial motifs, hard-stop linear gradients for kilim-like bands, and a few pseudo-elements for the parts that need a second layer. Each one is named after a tradition it only resembles.
