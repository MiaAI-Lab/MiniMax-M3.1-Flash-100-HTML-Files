DESCRIPTION:
A rose window, cut and glazed on the fly. The petals are real lancets — an arc along the base and two quadratics meeting at a point — hung in concentric rings with dark leading between them, and every light is clickable so you can re-glaze it. The lights and petals controls rebuild the whole tracery, the sun angle sweeps a highlight across the glass, and New glass re-seeds every colour from the fourteen-colour medieval palette.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Lancet petal geometry from an arc base and two quadratic curves
- Concentric ring layout with alternating angular offsets
- Per-petal colour assignment from a seeded generator
- Leading drawn as a dark outline over every light
- Painted striations clipped to each petal shape
- Radial light sweep composited over the glass

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Cut the window. Change the number of petals and rings, reseed the glass, toggle the symmetry and the daylight, and click individual lights to re-glaze them.
