DESCRIPTION:
A neon-noir landing page dressed as a bleeding-edge trading terminal. The page leans on three classic cyberpunk signals — magenta/cyan chromatic split on the headline, a CRT scanline veil and a flickering frame — but keeps them disciplined with hairline rules, corner-bracket panels and a monospace grid of live market data. A self-typing shell transcript gives the bottom of the page a narrative payoff.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Two-layer clip-path pseudo-element glitch with stepped keyframes and chromatic text-shadow
- CRT scanlines via repeating-linear-gradient plus a steps() flicker overlay
- Masked perspective grid overlay (radial mask + linear-gradient grid)
- Corner-bracket panel decoration built from two 2px borders
- Button fill wipe using background-size 0% → 100% on a solid gradient layer
- Self-typing terminal transcript with colour-coded inline spans
- Inset "shadow" icon tiles and neon text-shadow bloom

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
The page simulates a live system. The order-flow panel continuously appends new trade lines, the clock updates every second, telemetry values jitter, and the terminal types its own output on load. Tier cards act as selectable options (click to outline and glow). No login, no audio, no external data — everything is generated locally.
