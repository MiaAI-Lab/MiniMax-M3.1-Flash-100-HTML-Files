Why everyone
teaches the
crane first

The crane is not the easiest model in the world; the cup is. But the crane is the first one that makes somebody look up from the table, because it stands up on its own and has a head and a tail, and you made that happen with your hands and no glue.

The sequence of folds is also a proof. Each step is a transformation of the previous one, and the reason it works is that the paper has memory: every crease you make is a hinge that a later fold can use. The square at the end of this page is the same square you started with, with thirty hinges in it.
