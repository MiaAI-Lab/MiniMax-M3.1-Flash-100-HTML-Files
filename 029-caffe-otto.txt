TITLE: CAFFÈ OTTO — a small espresso bar

PROMPT:

Art direction: warm, dim, tactile — a marble counter at six in the evening. Palette: espresso-brown ground #1b1512, cream #f0e6d8, copper #c07a44, brass #e0b072, ink #2a1f19, dim #8d7a6b, and a single machine green #7dbf6a for the gauge's working band. Responsive: the hero stacks under 960px, the readout becomes 2×2 under 620px, the notebook cards stack and the menu drops its middle column.

DESCRIPTION:
An espresso machine you can operate, with the two things that matter rendered honestly: a real pressure profile and a cup that fills. The gauge is a conic-gradient dial with a green working band and a red over-pressure band, and the needle tracks the lever's actual behaviour — fast rise, slow decay, a hold at 5.6 bar in the last third. Steam drifts up the page the entire time. Everything else is a quiet bar: brass, cream, and a menu with prices in euros.

One button, one ritual. Pulling a shot runs a timed extraction with a realistic pressure curve, fills the cup, updates four readouts, and ends with a verdict in the hint line. The steam never stops. Nothing else is interactive — the rest of the page is a menu to read.
