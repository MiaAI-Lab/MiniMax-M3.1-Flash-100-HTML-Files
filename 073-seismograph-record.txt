DESCRIPTION:
A seismogram you can make happen. The record is built sample by sample — background microseism, a fast small P wave, a bigger slower S wave, then long surface waves — with the distance slider genuinely moving the arrivals apart and the magnitude slider scaling the amplitude on a logarithmic curve. Drum view redraws the same data as a spiral on a turning drum, with a pen at the end of the trace.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Sample-by-sample synthesis of P, S and surface-wave phases
- Logarithmic magnitude-to-amplitude scaling
- Spiral trace on a simulated rotating drum
- Glowing canvas trace with shadowBlur
- Monospaced phase table updated from the synthesised event
- Grid-paper recorder face from a canvas grid

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Make an earthquake. Move the magnitude, distance and gain, fire the named presets, and switch between the flat trace and the drum.
