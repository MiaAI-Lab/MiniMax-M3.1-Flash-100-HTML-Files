DESCRIPTION:
A heavy symmetric top, integrated properly. The precession rate comes out of the closed-form solution for a top with two conserved momenta, so the famous result falls out of the mathematics rather than being asserted: the faster you spin it, the slower it precesses. The disc is drawn in weak perspective from its true orientation, with the brass marker riding round it at the spin rate and the trace of the axis tip sweeping a circle behind.
