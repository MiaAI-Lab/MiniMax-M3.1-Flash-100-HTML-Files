Halcyon Hotel · 14 March · Room 903

The guest who never arrived

A man booked room 903 under the name of V. Auber, paid in cash, and was seen on the ninth floor at 23:40. The room was found empty at 06:00, the lift was at the ninth floor, and the river was four hundred metres away.
