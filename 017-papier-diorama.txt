Responsive: the editorial section stacks under 880px and the headline moves up.

DESCRIPTION:
A seven-sheet world you can take apart. Each hill is a torn clip-path edge in a different paper tone, the trees are stacked triangles, and the whole diorama parallaxes as you drag it — the nearest sheets travel furthest, the way real mounted paper does. The Evening button repaints the entire scene from a cream afternoon into a violet dusk with a single red accent, and Deepen exaggerates the separation until the construction is obvious.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Two-layer inline SVG feTurbulence paper texture (fractal tooth + stretched fibre) in multiply blend
- Hand-authored torn-edge clip-path polygons, one per paper layer
- Pointer-drag parallax with per-layer depth factors and eased inertia
- CSS triangle stacks for paper conifers (transparent borders)
- Border-radius arcs for birds drifting on long keyframe translations
- Hard offset shadows on buttons for a printed, physical feel
- Full-scene palette swap via a body class (day → evening)
- Hairline-ruled numbered method list

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Direct and tactile. Drag the scene to move the layers apart and watch the near sheets travel further than the far ones. The Deepen button scales the depth factors (Normal → Deepen → Flat) to exaggerate or collapse the depth. Evening repaints the scene. Reset eases the layers back to centre. The rest of the page is a static editorial.
