Interaction: Fire starts the firing and the marker climbs the curve while the readouts and the stage name update; Hold freezes the climb; Reset returns to 20 degrees; Atmosphere switches between oxidation and reduction; the two sliders rebuild the curve. Responsive: the two-column section stacks under 860px.

DESCRIPTION:
A firing schedule you can run. The curve is built from real segments — water smoking to 200 with a hold, a ramp through quartz inversion to 600, a controlled climb to the target with a soak, and a controlled cool — and the marker climbing it updates the elapsed time, the temperature, the ramp rate and the name of the stage the kiln is in. Move the target temperature and the whole curve changes shape underneath the cone lines.

MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES:
- Segment-based schedule with piecewise interpolation of temperature against time
- Dashed horizontal cone rules labelled with Orton bend temperatures
- Translucent gradient fill under the schedule curve
- Marker with halo and a dashed guide to the temperature axis
- Stage detection that names water smoking, burn-out, inversion, sintering, soak and glaze melt
- Clip-path kiln logo and ember-lit radial background

INTENDED INTERACTION MODEL:
Fire the kiln. Start the schedule, hold it at any point, reset it, change the atmosphere, and re-shape the curve with the target temperature and ramp rate sliders.
