The packing of atoms, drawn as real unit cells and rotated under the pointer. Switch between simple cubic, body centred, face centred, hexagonal close packed and diamond, and watch the coordination number and the packing fraction change with the geometry — because they are not decoration on the picture, they are consequences of where the spheres sit.
